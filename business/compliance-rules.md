# Compliance rules summary (a pre-screen guide, not legal advice; the firm's compliance department decides)

- **FINRA Rule 2210 (communications with the public):** fair and balanced; no false, exaggerated,
  unwarranted, promissory or misleading claims; no predictions or projections of performance; retail
  communications need registered-principal approval before use and must be retained.
- **SEC Marketing Rule (Advisers Act 206(4)-1):** testimonials, endorsements and third-party ratings only with
  required disclosures and oversight; performance advertising has strict presentation rules; no
  hypothetical performance to mass audiences.
- **Social media / LinkedIn:** static posts are retail communications; interactive replies are
  correspondence and must be supervised and archived through the firm's approved tools.
- **Email to clients and prospects:** use the firm's archived email system. CAN-SPAM for marketing email.
- **Privacy:** SEC Regulation S-P. Client personal information stays in approved systems.
- **Disclosures:** broker-dealer and adviser names, "Member FINRA/SIPC", BrokerCheck link, "investing
  involves risk, including the possible loss of principal" (exact wording from compliance: see firm-details.md).
- **Prohibited words and ideas:** guaranteed, safe, risk-free, can't lose, best/top/#1 without proof,
  promised returns, tax or legal advice.
