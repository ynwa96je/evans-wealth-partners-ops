# Pipeline stages (records live in the CRM, not in this repository)

1. **Prospect:** fits the ideal-client profile; not contacted yet.
2. **Contacted:** connection request, message or email sent by John.
3. **Engaged:** replied or interacted.
4. **Qualified:** need, timing and fit confirmed (sales-manager).
5. **Intro call booked** (Calendly).
6. **Discovery meeting.**
7. **Client** / **Not now** (follow-up date) / **Not a fit**.

Follow-up rhythm: no reply after a first message → one follow-up after 7 days, one more after 21 days, then stop.
