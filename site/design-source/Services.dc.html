Where every client starts

Financial planning

Your plan is a written document that pulls everything into one place: what you earn, spend, own and owe, and what you want. It shows whether your goals are on track and what to change if they aren't.

What we look at

  • Cash flow, spending and savings rate
  • Your goals, with dates and price tags
  • Your tax position, alongside your CPA
  • Your business and household balance sheets

What you get

  • A written plan and a one-page summary
  • A short list of next steps, in order
  • A yearly review, and updates when life changes

When can you stop?

Retirement planning

We build your retirement around the life you want, then test it against good markets and bad ones. You'll know how much room you have before you hand in your notice.

What we look at

  • Your target date and expected spending
  • When to claim Social Security
  • 401(k), IRA and pension choices
  • Health cover before Medicare starts at 65

What you get

  • A retirement date range you can plan around
  • A savings target for each year until then
  • A Social Security claiming recommendation

Money with a job to do

Investments

We invest to your timeline and to the level of risk your plan needs, not to the headlines. Your accounts are managed together, so each investment sits where it is taxed least.

What we look at

  • The risk you need to take, and the risk you can live with
  • What belongs in taxable, IRA and Roth accounts
  • Fund costs and fees
  • Large single holdings, including company stock

What you get

  • A written investment policy statement
  • Regular rebalancing back to your targets
  • Reports you can read in five minutes

Cover for what would really hurt

Protection strategies

Insurance should fill specific gaps in your plan. We review what you already own before suggesting anything new, and we size any new cover to real numbers.

What we look at

  • Life cover to replace income and clear debts
  • Disability cover for your earning power
  • The cost of long-term care
  • Buy-sell funding and key person cover

What you get

  • A review of every policy you already hold
  • A list of gaps, in dollars
  • Options compared side by side when cover is needed

A paycheck from your savings

Income strategies

When the salary stops, someone has to decide which account pays the bills each month. We plan that order so more of what you saved stays yours.

What we look at

  • Which accounts to draw from first
  • Roth conversions in lower-tax years
  • Required minimum distributions
  • Where an annuity might fit, and where it doesn't

What you get

  • A monthly income map for your first ten years
  • A tax plan for your withdrawals
  • A cash reserve, so a bad market never forces a sale

Passing on what you've built

Estate planning

We aren't attorneys and we don't draft legal documents. We work with your estate attorney so your plan, your paperwork and your accounts all say the same thing.

What we look at

  • Beneficiaries on every account and policy
  • Wills, trusts and powers of attorney
  • Connecticut estate and gift tax
  • Succession for a family business

What you get

  • A one-page map of who receives what
  • A beneficiary check on every account
  • One plan shared with your attorney and CPA

Evans Wealth Partners does not provide legal or tax advice. We work alongside your attorney and CPA.

For business owners

Business planning

Your business is probably your biggest asset and your biggest risk. We plan it alongside your household, so a decision in one never quietly costs the other. John has run a pub, a health club and a coaching business. He'll talk to you like an owner, because he was one.

Employee plans and benefits

401(k), SEP IRA and SIMPLE IRA plans, and the benefits package around them, chosen for your size, your staff and your budget.

Cash management

Operating cash, reserves and surplus kept in the right places, so the business has what it needs and spare cash isn't left sitting idle.

Risk management

A clear look at what could hurt the business, from liability and property to losing a key person, and how each risk is covered.

Business structure and tax efficiency

Whether your LLC, S corporation or partnership still fits, and how you pay yourself, worked out with your CPA.

Buy-sell agreements

An agreement between owners that says what happens if one dies, becomes disabled or leaves, and the money to carry it out.

Succession and exit planning

A plan for handing the business to family, a partner or your team, or for selling it, years before the day comes.

Liquidity events

When the business sells or a large payout lands, a plan for the taxes, the investments and what you'll live on afterwards.

Key person insurance

Cover that protects the business if an owner, or someone it can't easily replace, dies or becomes seriously ill.
