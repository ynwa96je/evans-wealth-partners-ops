John's story

From behind the bar to beside the client.

I grew up in England and took on my first pub at 21. At the time that made me the youngest publican in England. Running a pub teaches you cash flow fast, because the bills arrive whether the regulars do or not.

After that I founded Xtra Time, a youth soccer coaching business in Cardiff, for which I received an Entrepreneurial award from the Welsh Assembly, and later worked as a regional sales director with Major League Soccer. Here in Connecticut I owned The Zoo Health Club in Simsbury and received an official citation from the General Assembly in recognition of becoming a citizen and owning and operating a business in the true "American dream" fashion.

For more than seven years I have worked as a financial advisor at a bank, a broker-dealer and an insurance-affiliated firm. I started Evans Wealth Partners so that every client gets a plan first, and every recommendation can be traced back to it.

Most people call me Jack. You'll speak to me on your first call.
