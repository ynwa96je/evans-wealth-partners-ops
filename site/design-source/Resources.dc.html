Before we meet

What to bring to your first meeting

Don't have everything? Come anyway. We'll work out what's missing together.

  • Your last two tax returns
  • Recent statements for bank, brokerage and retirement accounts
  • Pay stubs, or your business's profit and loss statement
  • Life, disability and long-term care policies
  • Wills, trusts and powers of attorney, if you have them
  • A rough list of what you want the next ten years to look like
